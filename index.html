Drain Cleaning & Hydro Jetting

Stubborn clogs, grease lines and root intrusions cleared fast, with hydro jetting to scour pipes clean and keep them flowing.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Common Questions

Straight answers before you ever pick up the phone.

Do you really offer free slab leak detection?

Yes, slab leak detection is free with any repair we perform. We pinpoint the leak with electronic equipment and only open the exact spot that needs fixing.

Which water heater size does my home need?

We install 30, 40, 50, 75 and 100 gallon tank water heaters plus tankless systems. The right size depends on household size and peak hot water use, and we size it free during the estimate.

Do you handle whole house repipes?

Yes, whole house repipes are one of our specialties. We run new PEX or Type L copper lines and finish with full patching and texture, most jobs done in a single day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, AccuServe, Inc. is a C-36 Licensed Plumbing Contractor, license #996814. We are bonded and carry plumbing liability insurance, and we are insurance approved and handle home warranty repair work.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call or text 951-258-5030 and we will schedule a free in home plumbing estimate anywhere in our service area.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Corona, Eastvale, Norco, Chino, Chino Hills, Ontario, Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Montclair, Upland and the surrounding Inland Empire cities.

Do you service tankless water heaters?

Yes, we install and repair all major tankless brands and offer annual tankless flushes to keep water flowing strong and extend the life of the unit.
